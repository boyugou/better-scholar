See where you stand on every paper

A Chrome extension for Google Scholar profiles. It marks your authorship role and the venue's CCF / CORE rank on each paper, and adds up the result in one table: “4 first-author CCF-A papers”.

Every paper, coloured by your role

First, co-first, second and last author rows each get a colour, and your name is marked in the author line.

CCF and CORE ranks

The CCF 2026 and ICORE 2026 rank beside each venue, with the rule that matched it. A venue it cannot place stays unmarked: never a guess.

A role × rank table

Pick a cell to list just those papers, and copy the summary or the list for a CV or a grant form.

Awards and talks

Best papers, test-of-time awards, Oral, Spotlight and Highlight, from the authors' own words and from the official award pages of over 7,000 papers.

Cut-off author lists, completed

Scholar hides the last authors of long lists. One click reads them from the paper's page, or from Semantic Scholar if you allow it.

Duplicates merged

If you switch it on, entries that list one paper twice become one, with the citations added up.

Yours to shape

Choose what is shown, pick your own colours, hide the parts of Scholar's page you do not need.

Private by design

No account, no analytics, no tracking. Everything is computed in your browser, and the rank lists ship inside the extension.

Install

  1. Download the project from GitHub (the green Code button, then Download ZIP) and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click “Load unpacked” and choose the src folder. Then open any Google Scholar profile.

A Chrome Web Store listing may follow. Edge, Brave and other Chromium browsers work the same way.

Honest about its limits

Ranks are today's lists, not the rank at the time of publication, and the CCF list itself says it is not an evaluation. The extension never guesses a venue: what it cannot place is listed, not marked.
